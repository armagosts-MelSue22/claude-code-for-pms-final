Why your phone might have gone quiet

Two things about how the list gets built each time — one we're changing.

A "yes" and a "no" don't weigh the same

▲
Say yes
A little credit back toward getting called again
▼
Miss it or say no
A bigger ding — bigger than one "yes" can undo

One missed call costs more than one taken call earns back — so a rough stretch is easy to fall into and hard to climb out of on your own.

What climbing back out actually looks like today

It takes 7 clean, back-to-back accepted offers to climb from the quiet floor back to normal — because each "yes" only earns a little, and there's no other way up. And each of those 7 still has to be won first: being close enough, at the right moment, to even get the call. A single miss anywhere in that climb costs more than one win gains, so the real number is often higher than 7, not lower.

What happens during a quiet stretch — today vs. what we're building

Today

What we're building

A quiet stretch fades on its own
